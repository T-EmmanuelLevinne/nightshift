================================================================================
                NIGHTSHIFT: JOJO'S GAS STATION
        LIVE PRESENTATION SCRIPT — HOW THE MAIN CHARACTER PLAYS
================================================================================

PRESENTATION GOAL:
Explain and demonstrate how the player controls the main character and
experiences the story across all 5 Acts — moving from mundane, grounded
first-person tasks to escalating VHS-style psychological horror.

RECOMMENDED DEMO LENGTH: 5 to 8 minutes (depending on how much live gameplay
you show vs. narrate).

================================================================================
SECTION 1: OPENING & MAIN MENU (0:00 - 0:45)
================================================================================

[ON SCREEN]
- Launch the game and stay on the Main Menu for 10-15 seconds so the audience
  sees the eerie midnight gas station background, VHS scanlines, rolling
  tracking bar, and the glitching "NIGHTSHIFT: JOJO'S GAS STATION" title.
- Hover slowly over "START GAME" and "EXIT" to show the glitch snap & audio.

[SAY TO AUDIENCE]
"Good morning/afternoon everyone! Today we are presenting our first-person
psychological horror game built in Unity: 'NIGHTSHIFT: JOJO'S GAS STATION.'

Right away on the Main Menu, you can see our VHS analog-horror aesthetic —
with scanlines, flickering moonlight over the gas station, ambient horror
audio, and real-time VHS glitch effects across the title and interface.

In this game, you play as a lone night-shift convenience store clerk working
at Jojo's Gas Station along Route 9. What makes the gameplay unique is how
it uses ordinary, hands-on daily routines — cooking dinner, mopping floors,
scanning groceries, restocking shelves — to pull the player in, before slowly
twisting those same mechanics into horror.

Our story runs in a strict 5-Act alternating loop between two locations:
  - Act 1: At Home (Prologue)
  - Act 2: At the Gas Station (Night Shift 1)
  - Act 3: Back Home (Storm & Power Outage)
  - Act 4: Back at the Gas Station (Night Shift 2 & Climax)
  - Act 5: Home Finale (The Nightmare & Truth Reveal)"

================================================================================
SECTION 2: HOW THE MAIN CHARACTER PLAYS (CONTROLS & CORE MECHANICS) (0:45 - 1:30)
================================================================================

[ON SCREEN]
- Click "START GAME" (watch the heavy VHS glitch scramble fade into Act 1).
- As soon as you spawn outside the cabin, slowly look around and walk toward
  the front door while explaining the controls.

[SAY TO AUDIENCE]
"Before we jump deep into the story, here is how the main character plays:

1. Movement & Camera:
   The game is controlled in classic first-person perspective — W, A, S, D
   to walk, Left Shift to sprint, and the mouse to look around. We also added
   subtle retro VHS visuals, camera breathing, and immersive POV animations
   like waking up from bed, sitting in a chair, and washing your face.

2. Context-Sensitive Interaction (E Key):
   Whenever the main character looks at an interactive object — a door, a
   fridge, a stove, a coworker, or a customer's item — the center crosshair
   highlights and tells you the exact action you can take by pressing E.

3. 3-Slot Hotbar Inventory (Keys 1, 2, 3 or Mouse Wheel):
   At the bottom of the screen, the player manages up to three equipped items
   at once — such as food ingredients, a frying pan, a mop, a candle, a
   restock box, an umbrella, a multipurpose key, or their phone.

4. Hands-On Actions & Diegetic UI:
   Certain items have direct physical actions — like Left Clicking to mop
   floor stains or fold your umbrella indoors, or interacting directly with
   in-world screens like the TV remote, the Cashier PC register, and an
   interactive phone dialpad.

5. Objective Tracking:
   In the top-left corner, the player always has a clear Task list guiding
   them through each shift — though later in the game, even the task list
   starts reacting to the protagonist's sanity."

================================================================================
SECTION 3: ACT 1 — HOME PROLOGUE (GROUNDED ROUTINE) (1:30 - 2:45)
================================================================================

[ON SCREEN]
- Open the cabin front door and step inside.
- Open the fridge, grab Eggs and Bacon, place them on the kitchen counter.
- Open the cabinet for the Frying Pan, put it on the stove, turn the burner on,
  and fry 1 Egg and 2 Bacons (15-second cooking timer).
- Plate the cooked meal, place it on the dining table, grab a fork, sit in
  the dining chair, and eat.
- Wash the dirty plate at the sink, put it back, return the food to the fridge.
- Grab the TV remote and tune to Channel 5 (Breaking News).
- Turn off cabin lights, check that the front door is closed, and sleep.

[SAY TO AUDIENCE]
"Act 1 starts at the main character's cabin at night. Our protagonist comes
home exhausted and hungry, so the player physically goes through a realistic
evening routine:

- You open the refrigerator, grab eggs and bacon, place them on the kitchen
  counter, take a frying pan out of the cabinet, turn on the gas stove, and
  fry your meal for 15 seconds as the food visibly cooks and sizzles.
- Then you plate the meal, carry it to the dining table, grab a fork from
  the drawer, sit down, and eat in first-person POV — followed by washing
  your dirty plate at the sink and putting everything away.

Next, you pick up the TV remote and flip to Channel 5, where a Breaking News
broadcast reports a serious car accident in heavy fog right near Jojo's Gas
Station on Route 9. At this point, the character just shrugs it off as a
warning to be careful tomorrow — though this broadcast is actually major
foreshadowing for the ending.

Finally, the player turns off every light switch in the house, makes sure the
front door is shut, and lies down in bed to sleep before their night shift."

================================================================================
SECTION 4: ACT 2 — NIGHT SHIFT 1 AT JOJO'S GAS STATION (2:45 - 4:00)
================================================================================

[ON SCREEN]
- Talk to Billy outside/near the entrance for handover.
- Enter the store, equip the Mop from the hotbar, and Left Click to clean the
  3 floor stains.
- Serve Customer 1 (Steve): wait as he grabs a Coca-Cola from the fridge,
  stand behind the counter, scan the item with E, and check him out using
  the Cashier PC POS screen.
- Inspect the empty canned-food shelf.
- Serve Customer 2 (Melia) at the checkout counter.
- Go to the Stockroom, carry the Trash Bag out to the exterior Dumpster, and
  experience Junk's jumpscare encounter behind you.
- Return to the counter to end Shift 1.

[SAY TO AUDIENCE]
"In Act 2, the main character arrives at Jojo's Gas Station for Night Shift 1.
First, you talk to your coworker Billy for the shift handover — and quickly
discover he left the store dirty.

Here the player demonstrates the core job loop of the protagonist:
- Equipping the mop and scrubbing all three floor stains around the store.
- Running the cashier counter: Customers like Steve and Melia physically walk
  into the store, browse aisles, open the beverage fridge, pick up items,
  and bring them to the counter. The player steps behind the register, scans
  the barcode with E, and processes payment directly on our interactive
  Cashier PC point-of-sale interface.
- Checking store inventory: You notice the best-selling canned food shelf is
  completely empty and decide to restock it tomorrow.

Things first turn unsettling when you carry the trash bag out to the dumpster
behind the store. As soon as you toss it, a strange man named Junk appears
right behind you — freezing your movement until you turn around for a sudden
jumpscare and bizarre conversation about buying 'wet socks.' Shaken, the main
character returns to the counter and finishes the shift."

================================================================================
SECTION 5: ACT 3 — STORM, POWER OUTAGE & THE WINDOW (4:00 - 5:00)
================================================================================

[ON SCREEN]
- Wake up in the cabin during a heavy thunderstorm to loud knocking.
- Walk to the locked front door and talk to the stranger (Juan), refusing entry.
- Walk to the bathroom sink and press E to wash your face (eyes close fully,
  and power cuts out the exact moment they open).
- Test a dead light switch, find and light the candle from the living room,
  grab your phone from the bedroom, and call the Manager.
- React to the left living room window shattering -> inspect it -> lightning
  flashes to reveal the motionless Monster staring straight at you -> pan down
  to the dead pigeon.

[SAY TO AUDIENCE]
"Act 3 brings the main character back home during a violent thunderstorm.
You wake up slowly from bed to loud knocking on the locked front door. A
stranger named Juan begs to come inside, and the protagonist refuses.

Trying to shake off the uneasy feeling, the player goes to the bathroom sink
to wash their face. As the character splashes water and closes their eyes,
the entire house loses power right as their eyes open.

Now the player has to navigate in pitch darkness:
- Testing a light switch confirms the outage.
- You locate and light a candle in the living room, giving you a warm
  flickering light source as you search the bedroom for your phone.
- Calling your Manager, you're pressured into taking tonight's shift for
  double pay.

Right as you agree, the living room window suddenly shatters. When the player
investigates, a flash of lightning illuminates a dark, motionless figure
outside staring directly at you — before vanishing in the dark. Looking down,
you only see a dead pigeon on the floor, convincing the main character they
are just exhausted and imagining things."

================================================================================
SECTION 6: ACT 4 — NIGHT SHIFT 2, MURDER & STOCKROOM CLIMAX (5:00 - 6:30)
================================================================================

[ON SCREEN]
- Spawn outside the gas station in pouring rain holding the black umbrella.
- Walk inside, fold/sheath the umbrella (Left Click / F / R / Q), and place it
  in the Umbrella Stand behind the counter (which reveals your Phone on the
  counter basket).
- Pick up the Cardboard Restock Box, set it on the glowing restock zone, stock
  all 12 cans onto the shelf, and return the empty box to its original spot.
- Serve Steve (buys MamaChip from the chip shelf for $9).
- Let John into the Male Restroom (he locks it from inside).
- Serve Melia (buys Hershey's for $5, ominously whispers "Mortuus es.").
- Knock on the locked restroom door -> find the Multipurpose Key -> unlock the
  door to discover dead John in a pool of blood -> total blackout!
- Pick up your phone from the counter basket and dial 911 on the dialpad.
- Follow the trail of bloody footprints into the Stockroom -> door slams shut
  -> strobe-light Monster encounter -> open the door, walk fully outside ->
  2 seconds later, sudden lunging jumpscare cuts to black!

[SAY TO AUDIENCE]
"Act 4 is Night Shift 2 — and everything escalates rapidly.
You arrive in heavy rain holding an open black umbrella. Once indoors, you
close the umbrella and place it in the stand behind the counter, setting your
phone down on the counter basket.

Next, you tackle the unfinished restock job: carrying the cardboard box to
the aisle, stocking all 12 cans with a radial interaction prompt, and putting
the empty box back where it came from — if you forget, the protagonist keeps
reminding you every 5 seconds before the shift continues.

During the shift, three visitors arrive:
- Steve comes in with his umbrella and buys chips.
- A man named John begs to use the restroom to get out of the rain and locks
  himself inside.
- Then Melia returns, stays eerily silent at checkout, and whispers one Latin
  phrase before leaving: 'Mortuus es' — meaning 'You are dead.'

Worried about John being inside too long, the player knocks on the restroom
door, gets no answer, finds the Multipurpose Key, and forces it open —
discovering John dead in a pool of blood right before the station plunges
into a blackout.

The player rushes to the counter phone, dials 911 on the interactive keypad,
and waits for help — until corrupted audio hits and realistic bloody
footsteps lead straight into the Stockroom. Inside, the door slams shut
behind you, the overhead light strobes as the Monster creeps closer, and the
protagonist whispers 'Mortuus sum' — 'I am dead.' Even when the player
manages to open the Stockroom door and escape back outside, two seconds later
the Monster lunges out in a full jumpscare, cutting straight to black."

================================================================================
SECTION 7: ACT 5 — NIGHTMARE FINALE & ENDING REVEAL (6:30 - 7:15)
================================================================================

[ON SCREEN]
- Centered black-screen text: "Sumne re vera mortuus?" -> "Reality?"
- Player jolts awake in bed. Point out the corrupted top-left task:
  "Task: Wash your face / - Severe your head."
- Open the bedroom door and step into the hallway -> movement slows down
  until completely paralyzed -> cabin lights flicker wildly -> final Monster
  jumpscare -> centered black-screen ending reveal -> returns to Main Menu.

[SAY TO AUDIENCE]
"Finally, in Act 5, the main character jolts awake back in their bed, trying
to convince themselves it was all just a nightmare. Even the top-left HUD
begins to break down, twisting 'Wash your face' into 'Severe your head.'

As the player opens the bedroom door and tries to walk down the hall, your
movement speed progressively drains to zero until you're completely paralyzed.
The cabin lights flicker violently, the Monster lunges one last time, and the
screen cuts to black for the final reveal — tying everything directly back to
the Channel 5 Breaking News from Act 1:

  'You. Are. Dead.
   You got into a car accident.
   Near Jojo's station.
   Mortuus es... id non animadvertisti.'
   ('You are dead... you didn't realize it.')

After the reveal, the game loops cleanly back to the Main Menu."

"To wrap up, 'NIGHTSHIFT: JOJO'S GAS STATION' combines grounded first-person
simulation mechanics — cooking, cleaning, cashier POS checkout, restocking,
and phone dialing — with atmospheric VHS horror, scripted AI customers, and
a psychological twist that reframes every act you just played.

Thank you for watching our presentation! We'd be happy to answer any
