# Project Guidelines & Memory Rules

## CRITICAL SCENE OBJECT PROTECTION:
- **DO NOT TOUCH, MOVE, REBUILD, DESTROY, OR MODIFY `Beverage_Service_Counter`!**
- The user is manually positioning, arranging, and customizing the Beverage Service Counter, espresso machines, ice cream machines, cups, trays, and props in the scene.
- **NEVER** include `Beverage_Service_Counter` in any scene building, stocking, alignment, or cleanup scripts.
- **NEVER** run any script or code that deletes, recreates, repositions, or resets `Beverage_Service_Counter`.
- Treat `Beverage_Service_Counter` and all user-placed scene objects as strictly immutable and read-only.

## GAME STORYLINE & SCENE PROGRESSION:
The game follows a strict 5-part alternating story loop:
1. **Act 1: House Scene** (Prologue / Opening story & dialogue at the cabin before work)
2. **Act 2: Gas Scene** (`GasScene2` - Shift 1: Billy handover dialogue, tasks, cleaning, customer encounters)
3. **Act 3: House Scene** (Return Home: New dialogue and mid-game story progression at the cabin)
4. **Act 4: Gas Scene** (`GasScene2` - Shift 2: New story segment, escalations, and dialogue)
5. **Act 5: House Scene** (Finale: Final story chapter, climax, and the ending)
